INTERPRETATION
Observed agreement is well below what sampling noise alone would produce.
States rate different categories differently, so state 'generosity' does
not reduce to one generous-to-harsh dimension. This also explains why the
restaurants-only check (Q4) agrees with the full ranking only moderately.

This measures consistency across category baskets, not stability over
time or across reviewers. The noise ceiling is approximate: it squares the
mean SE instead of averaging squared SEs, approximates half-sample noise
by sqrt(2) despite unequal category weights, compares a rank correlation
with a variance-based quantity, and breaks rank ties by order. Treat the
gap as descriptive.

Spearman-Brown reliability of the FULL 66-category ranking: +0.616

The spread reduction comes from two steps: restricting to the 66 common
categories (0.1750 -> 0.1450) and reweighting them (0.1450 -> 0.0846).
A reliability near 0.6 means the standardized ranking is only broadly
informative. It is not a league table, and telling specific neighbouring
states apart would need pairwise intervals.
