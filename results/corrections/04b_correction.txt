NOTES ON B3-B6

B3-B6 require a full 12-month window before the end of the data.

The B4 contrast weighted by responders (did_att) is descriptive, not an
average causal effect. Matching uses broad pre-rating bins and calendar
years, and parallel trends are not demonstrated. Requiring five post-period
reviews selects on later activity. B5 pools calendar years within each
rating bin, so it is a less adjusted breakdown, not an exact decomposition
of B4.

B6 defines targeting from the whole review history, including the outcome
period, so it cannot show what would happen under a particular targeting
policy. Follow-up availability is calendar coverage, not proof that every
review or reply was captured.

Replies dated before their review are excluded from the valid-lag median.
Edited reviews are a possible explanation, not a verified one.
