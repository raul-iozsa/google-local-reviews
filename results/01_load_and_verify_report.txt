=== NOTES (added after the run above) ===
Check 4's "20 found" is the query's LIMIT 20, not a count. A COUNT(*) gives
14,070 duplicate (user_id, gmap_id, review_time) groups. The same JSON
review appears twice in the raw source file (checked in
review-Vermont.json.gz), so the duplicates come from the UCSD data, not
from sampling or loading.

The 75,544 rows with no rating (Check 3) also have no user_id, always both
together. They are third-party reviews syndicated into Google listings:
one is from a "Travelocity reviewer", dated 1999-12-31 at midnight, with
text that mentions October 2007. That is 0.73% of reviews_stratified.
Analyses that need a rating or a user exclude them with
user_id IS NOT NULL (the _clean views).

run_load_and_verify.py now counts Check 4 correctly and reports these rows
as Check 6.

61 reviews in reviews_global_clean are dated before 2005, some in 1990:
