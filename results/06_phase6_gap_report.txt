NOTES ON INTERPRETING THIS REPORT

EXTREME DISAGREEMENTS
The counts in that section are model-agreement counts, not verified
reviewer errors. "Read as negative by both models" means the text is hard
for both, not that the reviewer contradicted their own rating. The 5-star
taco review ("There asada fries are so fire" plus 33 flame emoji) is read
as negative by both models (BERT 2.18 stars), and it is plainly
enthusiastic. BERT is not an adjudicator.

BERT CHECK
BERT finding a smaller difference in the same direction as VADER shows
that the two methods agree on direction. It does not show that some
fraction of the gap is real: the ratio of two models' standardized effects
cannot estimate a proportion when they differ in scale and calibration.
The denominator is the SD of all observations across the four groups,
including between-group variation, not a within-group pooled SD, so each
contrast also depends on the other comparison's sample. For Test A (grim
topics) the two models point in opposite directions: VADER reads those
reviews colder and BERT marginally warmer.

HUMAN CHECK
The 450-review single-annotator audit (results/audit_v2) compares
categorical sentiment, with some AI discussion of items during labelling
and no second annotator. Its matched cold/warm panel is nearly all
positive, so it cannot settle differences in intensity, and it does not
cover the grim/reference contrast.

BASELINE ADJUSTMENT
Subtracting pooled rating/length means does not give every category or
state the same rating/length mix. If residual differences vary across
cells, composition still affects mean gaps, so these are baseline-adjusted
descriptive contrasts. The BERT check balances three broad length bands,
not exact lengths, and its extreme-category selection is post hoc.
Review-level standard errors are not cluster-robust.

LANGUAGE
Whether non-English reviews affect the state gap was not tested.
`compound == 0` is not a language detector: zero also comes from neutral
English or from cancelling valence, and non-English text can contain words
VADER knows.

Status: INCONCLUSIVE. Settling it needs explicit language identification
(with an "uncertain / too short" class), manual validation of a sample,
and the state contrast repeated on reliably English reviews.
[[CORRECTION:06_correction.txt:END]]
